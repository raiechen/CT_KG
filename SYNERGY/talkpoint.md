*• Let's talk about the problem we're solving.*

*• Today, we have what I call "sleeping data"—data that exists but isn't working for us.*

*• It has no structure, can't be retrieved easily, and sits isolated in various systems.*

*• Our scientists spend significant time on sample-specific analysis and manual reporting.*

*• Our solution is Application Driven Analysis—ADA.*

*apps for analysis.*

*• The goal: wake up that sleeping data and put it to work and speed up the analysis*
